Calendar overrides

Forces a specific schedule for a date, overriding the 511 holiday-calendar-derived determination used by /agency/{operator}/scheduletype (and the legacy /caltrain/scheduletype) and the departure tracker. Scoped per agency — Caltrain (CT) is the only agency this service tracks departures for today, but an override can be recorded for any agency. Re-submitting an existing agency/date edits it in place.
